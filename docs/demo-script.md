# Swarasahayi: demo video script (target 2:50, hard limit 3:00)

**Setup before recording**
- Record the live Vercel link on your phone in Chrome (screen recorder on). Quiet room, Malayalam voice installed, volume up.
- Profile: **Fish seller**, customer language **English** (best recognition). Reset with "Change" so the welcome flow is fresh.
- Do one dry run. If the mic misbehaves on camera, tap **Type instead** and say so honestly; do not hide it.
- Record voice-over in one take over the screen capture, or speak live. Upload to YouTube (public or unlisted), 3 minutes or less.
